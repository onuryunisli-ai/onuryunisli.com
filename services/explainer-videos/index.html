Overview

An explainer video takes something complicated — a product, an app, a banking service, a production process — and makes it clear in about a minute. Script first, then storyboard, then animation in 2D, 3D or a mix.

Explainers in this portfolio include Badamlı’s explainer videos and the nine-part SIRAB 75 series made at Element, which explained the water from its mineral composition to its production standards.

Questions

How long should an explainer video be?

60–90 seconds for most explainers, with 15–30 second cutdowns for social media.

2D or 3D?

2D is clearer for abstract ideas and services; 3D is better when a physical product or a process has to be seen. Mixing both is common.

Do you write the script?

Yes, or I refine yours. The script is approved before design begins.

Can we have other languages?

Yes. The animation is built so on-screen text and voice-over can be swapped for other markets.

How long does it take?

Usually three to five weeks, depending on length and style.

How is the price set?

A fixed quote based on length, style and the number of versions, within one working day of the brief.
