Questions

How long does a motion design project take?

A single 15–30 second animation usually takes one to two weeks. A full motion system or a series of social videos takes three to six weeks.

How is the price set?

Each project gets a fixed quote based on length, complexity and the number of versions. Send the brief and you will have it within one working day.

Do I get the source files?

Yes. After Effects project files and templates can be included, organised so your team can change text and swap footage without breaking the animation.

Can you work within our existing brand guidelines?

Yes. Most projects start from existing guidelines. If the brand has never been animated, the first step is to define how it moves — see logo animation.

Do you also do 3D?

Yes — product, packaging and environment work in Cinema 4D and Redshift. See 3D product animation.

Do you work white-label for agencies?

Yes. I produce under the agency’s name and never contact its clients directly. See white-label production.

How do we work across time zones?

I am in Baku, GMT+4. My afternoon overlaps the UK and European morning, and US feedback sent in the evening is usually addressed by the next morning.

2D or 3D motion?

2D motion graphics are faster and lighter and suit most social and explainer work. 3D adds material, light and depth for products, logos and launches. Many projects mix both.

Can you also make the social media versions?

Yes — see social media content. Cutdowns and formats are planned from the start, so they are not an afterthought.
