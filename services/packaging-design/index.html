Packaging design

Packs proven on a 3D shelf before they are printed.

Overview

Packaging is where a brand meets its buyer — at arm’s length, in a few seconds. The work covers label and pack design, range extensions and photoreal 3D visualisation, so a design is judged on a realistic bottle, can or pouch, not on a flat artboard.

Packaging in this portfolio includes the SIRAB rebrand and flavoured range, Vintage Badamlı for the brand’s 75th anniversary, Dinamit energy drink and JUIZ — made with Element — as well as ALIVE and LIVGRT HYDRO8.

Questions

Can we see the pack before printing?

Yes. Every direction is shown in photoreal 3D on the real bottle, can or pouch shape.

Can you design a whole range?

Yes — a system of colour, type and layout that lets new flavours and sizes join the family without a redesign.

Do you do limited and anniversary editions?

Yes. Vintage Badamlı brought back a 1970s–80s label for the brand’s 75th anniversary, with a premium gift box.

Do you prepare print-ready files?

Yes, prepared to your printer’s specification.

How long does packaging design take?

A single label usually takes three to six weeks including revisions; a full range takes longer.

How is the price set?

A fixed quote based on the number of SKUs and formats, within one working day of the brief.
