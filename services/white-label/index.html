Overview

I work as a white-label production partner for creative, branding and marketing agencies. You keep the client relationship; I produce motion, 3D, logo animation and AI films under your name, in your files and to your deadlines.

Since 2024 I have produced 3D and motion for an agency in the United States, alongside client work through Element in Baku. Agencies bring me in when the in-house team is full, for skills they do not keep in-house — 3D and AI video — and for pitch films that have to be ready by Monday.

Baku is GMT+4. My afternoon overlaps the UK and European morning, and US feedback sent at the end of your day is usually addressed by the time your team is back.

Questions

Will you contact our client directly?

No. All communication goes through you unless you ask otherwise, and nothing appears in my portfolio without your written permission.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, before the brief if you prefer.

Which tools and files do you work with?

After Effects, Cinema 4D, Redshift, Illustrator, Photoshop and current generative video tools. I work in your project templates and naming conventions.

Can you start at short notice?

Often, yes. Send the brief and the deadline and I will confirm availability within one working day.

How do you charge?

A fixed price per project, agreed before work starts, invoiced to the agency.

How do time zones work?

Baku is GMT+4. UK and European mornings overlap my afternoon; for US agencies, work moves forward overnight while your team is offline.
