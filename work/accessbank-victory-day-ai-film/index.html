Victory Day — AI Film

AccessBank | Victory Day

A 78-second AI film for 8 November, Victory Day.

A truck in the rain. A villager walking up to the truck with a crate of fruit. A friend's hand at the top of a cliff. And one quiet morning in Shusha.

The film is told entirely through one soldier's eyes. We never see his face, only the road and the people who were next to him on it.

Made with AI, finished like a film

I made the whole film with AI tools — Nano Banana Pro, Seedance 2.5, ElevenLabs and Claude AI: characters, locations, voices and every shot. Then I edited, graded and finished it in After Effects.

More on how I work: AI commercials and the AI tools I use.

Storyboard

The whole film, shot by shot, before a single frame was generated.

Elements

Before generating a single shot, I built everything the film needed as fixed references: 4 characters, 9 locations and sets, one truck and 4 props.

The hero is the only one without a face. His element is just his hands, sleeves and rifle, because the camera is his eyes.

Every shot prompt called them by their tag, like @char_comrade or @prop_bracelet. That's how the same friend, the same red bracelet and the same flag show up in every frame.
