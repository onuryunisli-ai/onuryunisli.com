Turkish Petroleum

Baku 2026 Exhibition Stand

TPAO at the Caspian Oil & Gas Exhibition

For the 31st International Caspian Oil & Gas Exhibition in Baku, Element designed and delivered the exhibition stand of Turkish Petroleum Corporation (TPAO). The stand received the “Most Creative Stand” award at the exhibition.

The concept

The design is inspired by the strategic vision of Sultan Mehmed the Conqueror. It combines contemporary architecture with immersive exhibition design to express TPAO’s energy vision.

From concept to build

Element ran the whole project — creative concept, design, 3D visualisation, production and on-site implementation. Working from detailed 3D visualisation means the stand, its materials and its screens are designed and approved before anything is built.
