Baku Resort & Spa

Hotel Brand Identity

A hotel identity rooted in Azerbaijani architecture

Element created the complete brand identity for Baku Resort & Spa, a hospitality destination in Kyrgyzstan: logo, visual identity, brand guidelines and a corporate identity system that keeps the guest experience consistent across every touchpoint.

The mark

The logo draws on Azerbaijani architectural heritage. Traditional arch forms and ornamental motifs are redrawn with a contemporary graphic approach, so the mark reflects the elegance of Azerbaijani culture while signalling a premium hospitality experience.

The system

The identity was designed to be timeless, recognisable and adaptable — a distinctive visual language that works across physical and digital environments, from signage and print to screens and motion.
