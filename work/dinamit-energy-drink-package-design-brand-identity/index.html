Dinamit

Package Design & Brand Identity

Dinamit®

Dinamit energy drink has been produced by Enna LLC since 2022. Dinamit is an energy drink developed on the basis of technologies that meet world standards to ensure customer and consumer satisfaction. Dinamit meets true energy drink standards with its taurine and caffeine content. The drink is prepared with "non-contact production principles" from the beginning of production to the customer. Dinamit energy drink is offered to its customers in 250 ml and 500 ml packaging.
