LAMINA DEKOR - BRAND IDENTITY

At Lamina Dekor, we believe that great design begins with exceptional materials.
Specializing in premium laminate flooring and high-quality kitchen countertops, we are dedicated to transforming everyday spaces into inspiring environments.

Our vision is simple yet ambitious — to set new standards of quality, innovation, and service in the decorative surfaces industry. We continuously invest in advanced technologies and carefully curated designs to ensure that every product we deliver reflects superior craftsmanship and aesthetic excellence.

Driven by a passion for perfection, we aim to provide our customers with not only beautiful and durable solutions but also an experience that exceeds expectations at every step. From initial concept to final installation, Lamina Dekor is committed to offering the highest level of customer satisfaction and long-term value.

Looking ahead, we aspire to expand our reach both locally and internationally, becoming a trusted name synonymous with quality, reliability, and contemporary style.​​​​​​​

Client | Dekor Group

Agency: Element VS

Baku, Azerbaijan | 2024

The logo is a combination of the letter “L” for “Lamina” and the letter “D” for “Decor”, forming diagonally arranged pieces of wood and crossed symbols.

The cross icon in the center of the logo represents precision in this area,

as it reflects the size and shape of the furniture.

The use of orange in the color choice creates a strong and eye-catching

image of the brand.

The details and colors in the logo reflect the dynamism and professionalism of the brand.
Overall, the logo emphasizes the brand image with a modern, trendy and

minimalist design.
